# Portfolio design direction

Audience: engineering leaders, identity teams, clients, and recruiters evaluating Swapnil's IAM and Auth0 work.

A gallery of identity engineering. The memorable visual is an original sculptural access aperture: concentric machined-silver rings around a cobalt center, with a subtle pointer-driven change in perspective. It reflects access boundaries without padlock clip art or an ambient network animation.

Palette: porcelain #f3f4f6, ink #181b22, cobalt #2445dc, silver #c7cbd4, slate #616875, white #ffffff. Manrope for navigation and body; Instrument Serif for large display titles. Left aligned, asymmetrical compositions, generous negative space, legible body copy. One deliberate opening animation; interaction-driven motion after that. No repeating reveal effects or cursor replacement.

The homepage progresses from an introduction to identity work, detailed career history, a filterable project archive, capabilities and credentials, and a contact form. Preserve CMS content, all project details, backend endpoints, and draft mode. Native details for experience and projects give keyboard-accessible disclosure. Contact stays functional and validates input.

References reviewed: https://www.alexanderhorner.com/work/portfolio-2026 for compositional variety; https://brittanychiang.com for clear career navigation; https://www.awwwards.com/websites/portfolio/ for portfolio art direction. Frontend guidance: https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design. These inform principles rather than copied layouts or assets.
